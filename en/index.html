02 / RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS

Research

How much can we learn about wildlife from things people already share online?

Google Scholar ↗

FEATURED PAPER · PLOS ONE · 2021

Passive citizen science

My collaborators and I tested whether public Flickr photos, image recognition and UK biodiversity records could help identify wildlife observations. We explored how everyday photos could complement existing recording schemes.
