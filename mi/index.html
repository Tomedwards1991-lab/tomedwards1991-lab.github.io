03 / TAKU CV

Ngā wheako mahi

He tīpakonga o aku tūranga i te rangahau, i te hanga pūmanawa rorohiko me te mātauranga.

  1. 2023–2026

    Kairangahau (Research associate)

    Te Whare Wānanga o Cardiff

    I āwhina au ki te waihanga i te pātaka kōrero reo rua e kīia nei ko te Wales Safeguarding Repository, i te taha o te Welsh Government me ngā hoa mahi o te kaupapa. Mā taua pātaka ka māmā ake te rapu pūrongo mā te hunga e mahi ana ki te tiaki i te tangata.

    Mō te pātaka kōrero ↗
  2. 2020–2024

    Kaipūkaha pūmanawa rorohiko matua

    Sirma

    I whakawhanake au i a Medrec:M, he tūāpapa hauora matihiko e taea ai te aroturuki i ngā tūroro me te whakawhiti kōrero ki a rātou mai i tawhiti. I hanga au i ētahi āhuatanga e whakamahi ana i ngā raraunga, ā, i ārahi hoki au i ētahi atu kaipūkaha.

  3. 2017–2020

    Kaipūkaha pūmanawa rorohiko

    Hut Six Security

    I hanga au i ngā pūmanawa rorohiko mō tētahi tūāpapa whakangungu haumaru ā-ipurangi. I āwhina hoki au ki te hanga rauemi ako e māmā ai te whakamahi mā ngā ākonga.

  4. 2015–2017
